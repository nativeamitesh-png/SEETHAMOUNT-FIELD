# Seethamount Field

Light phone recorder for the Seethamount farm: record photos, videos and voice notes, a contour map with your GPS spot, and tap to mark trees. Records stay on the phone's browser until you send the day file to yourself.

Contours and tree positions come from the 27 March 2026 survey and are shown as model, not measured. The survey frame is assumed WGS84 until 3 GPS points are walked.

Built from the Living Farm project. No night walks or events.
